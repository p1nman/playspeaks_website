The Office

Step inside.

A peaceful place to pause along the climb.

Welcome

A place to rest along the way

Healing happens best where you feel safe. Each room was set up to feel calm, warm, and welcoming — a place where children can play freely, teens and adults can talk openly, and families can take a breath. Take a look around.

Waiting Room

A moment to breathe

Settle in on the couch, grab a cup of coffee or tea, and take a breath before your session. Look closely and you’ll find a familiar view on the wall — a reminder that every mountain has a way through.

Adult & Adolescent Room

Room to talk, space to think

A comfortable, private space for teens and adults. Sit back and talk things through at your own pace — and when words are hard to find, shelves of sand tray miniatures offer another way to tell your story.

Play Therapy Room

Where play speaks

Play is a child’s natural language. This room is filled with the toys, art, and imaginative spaces children need to express what they may not yet have words for — from the play kitchen and dollhouse to a cozy seat beneath the tree.

play (verb)

  1. to engage in activity for enjoyment and recreation
  2. the act of healing
